    What is NextDorm?

    Sharing-economy circulation, built for campus life.

    Every move-in and move-out, Brown students buy things they'll use for a semester and then toss, donate off-campus, or leave on the curb. NextDorm turns that churn into a closed loop: list what you don't need, find what you do, and circulate it all within a verified campus community.

    No app store required — NextDorm installs straight from your browser, so it's always one tap away.

    Features

    Everything you need to circulate, nothing you don't

    List & borrow in seconds

    Snap a photo, set your terms, and your item's visible to every verified student on campus.

    Zero-waste move-out

    Move-out week becomes a giveaway, not a dumpster run — list a whole room in minutes.

    Verified Brown community

    Every account is tied to a Brown identity, so you always know who you're circulating with.

    Real-time chat

    Coordinate pickup times and details without ever leaving the app.

    How it works

    Three steps to circulation

    1

    List it

    Snap a photo of what you're giving up, lending, or looking for.

    2

    Match it

    NextDorm connects you with a verified student nearby in minutes.

    3

    Circulate it

    Meet up, hand it off, and keep one more thing out of the landfill.
